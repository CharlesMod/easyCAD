Privacy policy

easyCAD · effective 3 November 2026

easyCAD is a desktop application. Your designs are files on your own computer. easyCAD has no servers of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send your data to its developers or to anyone else.

Google Drive sync (optional)

Sync is off until you choose Sign in with Google in easyCAD's settings. When you sign in:

  • What easyCAD can access. It asks for one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: access to files that easyCAD itself created in your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. It also reads your account's name and e-mail address from Drive (to show who is signed in), only on your computer.
  • What it does with it. It copies your easyCAD documents, parts library and easyCAD settings to an easyCAD folder in your My Drive, and copies changes made on your other computers back. Files it removes go to your Drive trash, where you can restore them for 30 days.
  • Where your data goes. Only between your computer and your own Google Drive, directly over HTTPS. Nothing passes through any server run by easyCAD's developers.
  • Your sign-in. The Google sign-in token is stored in your operating system's keychain (Keychain, Credential Manager or the Secret Service) on your computer and is used only to talk to Google Drive.
  • Stopping. Sign out in easyCAD revokes the token at Google and deletes it from your computer. You can also remove easyCAD's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your files stay in your Drive and on your computer until you delete them.
easyCAD's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the sync feature you turned on; it is not sold, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/CharlesMod/easyCAD/issues.
